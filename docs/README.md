## What is AI and IBM Z { #what-is-ai }

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming how organizations create value by turning data into actionable insights, agentic workflows, and decision support. IBM Z, the industry's leading enterprise computing platform, provides the performance, security, resiliency, and scalability required to run mission-critical workloads while bringing AI directly to enterprise data and transactions, and also using AI to simplify operations of IBM Z. AI on IBM Z features state-of-the-art hardware and software capabilities designed to optimize AI at scale alongside your enterprise's most critical workloads and data. This includes hardware accelerators such as the IBM Telum on-chip inference accelerator, a broad portfolio of IBM software, open-source frameworks, and an ecosystem of ISV solutions for specific enterprise use cases.

Together, AI and IBM Z enable organizations to accelerate innovation across two complementary domains: **AI for Business** and **AI for Operations**. By combining these two categories, IBM Z helps enterprises unlock the full potential of AI across both business processes and technology operations.

### AI for Business

AI for Business focuses on driving business outcomes through capabilities such as Transactional AI, where AI is embedded into real-time business transactions to improve customer experiences, detect fraud, personalize interactions, and optimize decisions at the point of execution.

### AI for Operations

AI for Operations focuses on improving the management and efficiency of the IT environment itself, using AI to streamline operations, predict and prevent issues, enhance system performance, strengthen security, assist with DevOps, and increase operational resilience.
